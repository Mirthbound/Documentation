# BabyTalk Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** \ September 2, 2025

BabyTalk (“the App”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how it is used, and your choices as a user.

---

## Information We Collect

### 1. Information You Provide

* **Child profile data:** You may enter your child’s name, birthdate, or developmental notes. These stay **on your device only** and are not shared with us.
* **Audio recordings:** If you record your child’s speech, the files are stored **locally on your device**. They are not uploaded or transmitted.

### 2. Automatically Collected Information

* **App usage data:** We use third-party services (such as Google Mobile Ads and User Messaging Platform) that may collect anonymized usage data to provide advertising and consent management.
* **Identifiers:** Advertising frameworks may use a unique identifier to show relevant ads if you grant permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt.

---

## How We Use Information

* To provide you with core features (tracking milestones, storing recordings, showing resources).
* To display **ads** in a small banner format. Ads help support the App but may be removed permanently via a **one-time in-app purchase**.
* To improve the user experience and ensure the App functions as expected.

---

## Data Storage and Security

* All personal data and recordings remain **on your device**.
* We do not collect, store, or sell your personal data.
* Third-party ad providers may collect limited, anonymized data as described in their own privacy policies.

---

## Your Choices

* **Ads:** You can disable ads permanently by purchasing the in-app upgrade (“Remove Ads Forever”).
* **Tracking:** You may decline tracking when prompted by iOS. Ads will still appear, but they may be less personalized.
* **Recordings:** You can delete any audio files directly from within the App.

---

## Children’s Privacy

BabyTalk is intended for use by **parents and caregivers**, not by children directly. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

---

## Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, which have their own privacy policies:

* **User Messaging Platform (UMP)**: [Google Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
