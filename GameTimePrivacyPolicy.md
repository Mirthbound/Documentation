# GameTime Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 25, 2025_

GameTime ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you use the GameTime iOS application (the "App").

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 User-Provided Information
- **Game Library Data**: Titles you add to your library, the list status (Backlog, Playing, etc.), personal ratings, playtime, notes, and start/end dates.
- **Preferences**: Default tab, default sort/list, rating scale, and other in-app settings stored to personalize your experience.

### 1.2 Automatically Collected Information
- **App Analytics (Optional)**: Basic diagnostic information provided by Apple if you opt in to share analytics with developers (e.g., crash logs, device type, OS version). We do not collect this directly; it is provided through Apple’s developer tools if enabled.

### 1.3 Third-Party Data
- **IGDB Game Data**: When you search or browse within the Discover tab, we fetch game metadata (covers, ratings, release dates, platforms, etc.) from the Internet Game Database (IGDB) using Twitch/IGDB credentials. This data is retrieved via secure API calls and is not linked to your identity.

We **do not** collect personal information such as your name, email address, or payment details within the App.

---

## 2. How We Use Information

We use the collected information to:
- Maintain and display your game library, statistics, and preferences.
- Provide discovery features such as search, curated lists, and similar game suggestions.
- Sync your library across devices via iCloud/CloudKit.
- Improve app reliability and diagnose issues (e.g., using anonymized crash reports from Apple, if available).

---

## 3. Data Storage & Sync

- **On-Device Storage**: Your library data is stored locally on your device using Core Data/UserDefaults for offline access.
- **iCloud / CloudKit**: If you are signed in to iCloud and have iCloud Drive enabled, GameTime syncs your library data to your private CloudKit database. This keeps your data in sync across devices using the same Apple ID. We do not have access to your CloudKit data.
- **Third-Party APIs**: Requests to IGDB are stateless and only transmit the search terms or game IDs needed to fulfill the request. No personally identifiable information is sent to IGDB.

---

## 4. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do **not** sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Data sharing is limited to:
- **iCloud/CloudKit**: syncing between your own devices.
- **Apple**: optional diagnostic/crash reports if you opt in via iOS settings.
- **IGDB**: retrieval of public game metadata only.

We may disclose information if required to comply with legal obligations or to protect our rights and users’ safety.

---

## 5. Data Retention & Deletion

- Your data remains on your devices and iCloud until you delete it.
- To remove your data, you can:
  - Delete entries directly in the App.
  - Remove the App from your device (which clears local data).
  - Delete the App’s data from iCloud via Settings → Apple ID → iCloud → Manage Storage.

---

## 6. Children’s Privacy

GameTime is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such data, please contact us so we can remove it.

---

## 7. Security

We use Apple-provided frameworks (CloudKit, Keychain, UserDefaults) which implement industry-standard security. While we take reasonable measures to protect your data, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

---

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect product or legal changes. We will update the “Last updated” date above when changes occur. Significant changes may be communicated via in-app messaging or release notes.
